# Home (/[locale]) — IA-ZR agency site

Scope: home page, both locales. Mode: Persuade. Audience and job: PRODUCT.md (two equal buyers). Action: book a consultation (booking drawer); secondary: WhatsApp / form.

## Direction contract
THESIS: An agency page that proves its systems working, not a portfolio mood. It refuses the dark neon AI-agency default (glow, glass, gradient text) and the dark-only world it replaces.
OWN-WORLD: Two-tone cobalt on sand: ground #e6dbc7, surface #ddd0b9, ink and accent cobalt #062b8a, muted #3253a4, one inverse cobalt band with sand ink. Host Grotesk 500 display at -0.035em, 400 text; JetBrains Mono only for data in UI windows. Soft radius 8/12/20, 1px ink hairlines at 14%, opaque surfaces, tonal buttons, wordmark "IA-ZR". Real full-colour tool icons. WebGL as cobalt ink particles, normal blending, no bloom.
STORY: Visitor sees AI resolving real operations, recognises their own problem, picks Track 01 or Track 02, watches the end-to-end flow, checks 9 real deployments, tries the assistant, meets the founder, books.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Centred hero: h1 "IA y automatización para empresas" (EN "AI and automation for businesses") 72px on the page axis, a lead of two to three lines, tonal primary "Agendar diagnóstico" + secondary "Ver los dos servicios" (renamed from "Ver las dos vías": the page calls the tracks services), then the hard fact as a 14px line under the actions (9 systems in production · US and LATAM; moved out of a pill above the h1 to honour the craft floor's eyebrow ban), then a lit frame across the wrap holding the IA-ZR Control window whose operations feed loops (WhatsApp answered, appointment booked, 5-star review via NFC/QR, lead in CRM), its sidebar labelled by channel; the cobalt particle entity sits behind the frame, never behind the words, and is gone before the frame fades.
FORM: Taste centred hero (x.ai pattern) with surface-pairs rhythm, close-word footer, nav fixed opaque. seed: none (pinned) — user approved "Claro cobalto-arena" as proposed: palette cobalt-on-sand, Host Grotesk + JetBrains Mono, centred hero with the operations window, WebGL limited to hero and the cases frame.
Signature interaction: the cases frame — selecting one of the 9 deployments swaps a WebGL screen inside the frame (depth travel + liquid hover); the flow section's four steps, click-driven, re-render the product window.
FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
